## Notes

Serves 2. About 30 min. (Add the rice powder and herbs just before serving.)

## Ingredients:

- 300g (11 oz) ground chicken, not lean (dark meat preferred)
- 30ml (2 Tbsp) water or unsalted chicken stock
- 25ml (1 Tbsp + 2 tsp) fish sauce, divided (1 tsp for cooking)
- 1 small shallot, thinly sliced (about ¼ cup)
- 30ml (2 Tbsp) lime juice
- 1–2 tsp roasted chili flakes, to taste
- 2 Tbsp toasted rice powder (below)
- 6–8 sprigs cilantro
- 2–3 leaves sawtooth coriander (optional, or more cilantro)
- 1 green onion, chopped
- ⅓ cup mint leaves, torn if large
- Sticky rice and crunchy vegetables, to serve

### Toasted rice powder:

- 3 Tbsp uncooked sticky or jasmine rice
- 1 makrut lime leaf (optional)

## Instructions:

1. Toast rice and lime leaf in a dry pan on high, stirring constantly, until deep brown. Grind to a powder, keeping some grit.
2. Bring water or stock to a simmer in a wide pot. Add chicken and 1 tsp fish sauce, stir to break up until just cooked.
3. Off heat, add shallot and stir to wilt slightly.
4. Add remaining fish sauce, lime juice, chili flakes, and 2 Tbsp rice powder. Stir in green onion and cilantro.
5. Taste. If too sharp, add ½–1 tsp sugar or more rice powder.
6. Top with mint. Serve warm or room temp with sticky rice and vegetables.
