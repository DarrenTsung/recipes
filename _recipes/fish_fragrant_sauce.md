## Adapted from: Woks of Life, Eggplant with Garlic Sauce (魚香茄子)

Storable seasoning mix for [eggplant with garlic sauce](/eggplant_with_garlic_sauce). Makes 4 servings (~360g) — use ¼ (~90g) per batch, store the rest in the fridge. Hot water, cornstarch, and aromatics get added fresh when cooking (see the recipe).

## Ingredients:

- 4 tablespoons sugar (48g)
- 6 tablespoons oyster sauce (108g)
- 4 tablespoons rice vinegar (60g)
- 4 tablespoons Shaoxing wine (60g)
- 4 tablespoons light soy sauce (72g)
- 4 teaspoons toasted sesame oil (18g)
- 2 teaspoons dark soy sauce (12g)

## Instructions:

1. Warm slightly to dissolve the sugar if needed, then combine all ingredients. Store in the fridge.
