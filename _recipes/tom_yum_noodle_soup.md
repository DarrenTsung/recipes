Adapted from: <https://thewoksoflife.com/tom-yum-noodle-soup-kuay-teow-tom-yum/> (simplified with nam prik pao in place of chili flakes, chili vinegar, and garlic oil)

## Notes

Serves 2.

(Tweaked to simple pork meatballs instead of loose ground pork after first try)

## Ingredients:

### Broth:

- 5 cups pork or chicken stock
- 1 stalk lemongrass, cut into 3-inch pieces and split lengthwise
- 5 cilantro roots/stems
- 6 fish balls or 4 oz sliced fish cake (optional)

### Per bowl:

- 1 Tbsp nam prik pao (to taste — same Maepranom one as [tom yum goong](/tom_yum_goong))
- 1 Tbsp fish sauce
- 1½ Tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp sugar (nam prik pao is already sweet, so less than the original 2 tsp)
- Splash of white vinegar (optional, stands in for the chili vinegar)
- 1 Tbsp chopped roasted peanuts
- 1 Tbsp chopped scallion
- 1 Tbsp chopped cilantro

### Meatballs:

- 8 oz ground pork
- 1 tsp fish sauce
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- ¼ tsp white pepper
- 1 Tbsp cold water

### Noodles:

- 8 oz dried thin rice noodles (pho-style)

## Instructions:

1. Mix meatball ingredients and stir hard in one direction until sticky, ~1 min. Roll into ~1-inch balls with wet hands.
2. Simmer stock with lemongrass and cilantro roots. Drop in meatballs and fish balls; simmer until meatballs float and are cooked through, ~5 min. Cover and keep warm.
3. Boil a large pot of water and cook noodles until al dente.
4. While noodles cook, add the per-bowl seasonings to each bowl. Loosen the nam prik pao with a splash of hot broth.
5. Add noodles, ladle over broth, meatballs, and fish balls. Mix and taste; adjust with more nam prik pao, lime, or fish sauce.
