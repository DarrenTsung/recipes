---
title: Lemongrass Marinated Chicken
category: Mains
---

## Ingredients:

- 4 chicken thighs (skin-on, bone-in)
- 50g oyster sauce
- 15g fish sauce
- 15g honey
- 2-3 Bird's Eye chilies
- 1 lemongrass cube
- Garlic powder

## Instructions:

1. Debone chicken thighs, keeping skin on.
2. Combine chicken with the rest of the ingredients in a bag.
3. Remove chicken from bag and pick off lemongrass chunks before cooking.
