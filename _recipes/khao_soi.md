## Source

## Notes

Northern Thai curry noodle soup. Drumsticks simmer about 40 min.

## Ingredients:

### Curry paste:

- 2 pods black cardamom (seeds only)
- 1½ Tbsp coriander seeds
- 0.75 oz ginger, thinly sliced
- 1½" fresh turmeric (about 5g), thinly sliced (or 1 tsp ground)
- ½ cup shallots, sliced ¼" thick
- 0.75 oz mild dried chilies (guajillo or puya)
- 1 tsp coarse salt

### Soup:

- 1½ cups coconut milk, plus extra for drizzling
- 8 chicken drumsticks
- 3 cups water
- 3 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp black soy sauce
- 2–3 tsp fish sauce, as needed
- 1 Tbsp palm sugar, finely chopped (or brown sugar)
- 12 oz flat fresh egg noodles (wonton noodles), 3 oz of it for frying
- Oil for frying
- Chopped cilantro and/or green onions

### Condiments:

- 1 lime, cut into wedges
- ¼ cup chopped shallots or red onion
- ½ cup chopped pickled mustard greens
- Fried chili flakes, to taste

## Instructions:

1. Paste: toast cardamom and coriander seeds in a dry skillet on high until the coriander darkens slightly. Char ginger and turmeric slices 2–3 min per side. Char shallots until spotted.
2. Stem the chilies, cut into chunks, shake out most seeds. Grind with cardamom and coriander to a powder.
3. Pound ginger and turmeric to a fine paste in a mortar. Add shallots and salt, pound. Add the ground spices, pound to a fine paste.
4. Boil ½ cup coconut milk on medium. Add paste, stir several minutes until very thick and the oil sizzles at the edges.
5. Add remaining coconut milk, water, both soy sauces, palm sugar, and drumsticks. Simmer gently on low about 40 min until fork tender. Adjust with fish sauce or salt (add water if too salty).
6. Crispy noodles: cut the 3 oz noodles to about 4". Fry small handfuls in 2" of oil at 350°F until golden and the bubbling almost stops. Drain.
7. Boil the remaining noodles (about 3 min, check the package), toss with 1 Tbsp oil, divide into bowls.
8. 1–2 drumsticks per bowl, ladle over broth, drizzle 1–2 Tbsp coconut milk, top with crispy noodles and cilantro. Serve with the condiments.
