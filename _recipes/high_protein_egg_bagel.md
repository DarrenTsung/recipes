## Source

<https://www.skinnytaste.com/high-protein-egg-bagel/>

## Ingredients:

(makes 4)

- 177g (1¼ cups) all-purpose flour (or whole wheat / gluten-free 1-to-1)
- 2 tsp baking powder (fresh)
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- ¾ cup nonfat Greek yogurt, drained (thick kind like Fage or Stonyfield, Chobani is too thin)
- 2 large eggs, separated (yolks for dough, whites beaten for brushing)
- Toppings: everything bagel seasoning, sesame, poppy seeds, etc.

## Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line baking sheet with parchment or silicone mat.
2. Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt.
3. Mix in yogurt and yolks with a fork until crumbly.
4. Knead on lightly floured surface 15-20 times until smooth and tacky (add flour if sticky).
5. Divide into 4. Roll each into a ~¾" rope and join ends.
6. Brush with egg white, add toppings.
7. Bake on top rack 25 min (or air fryer 280°F, 15-16 min). Cool 15 min before slicing.

(keeps 1 day room temp, 3 days fridge, 3 months frozen sliced)
