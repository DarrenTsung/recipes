## Notes

Serves 4. About 40 min. Wrap in lettuce or perilla leaves with ssamjang. (A commenter's chicken didn't cook through with everything in at once; brown the chicken first if the pieces are big.)

## Ingredients:

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- 8 oz green cabbage
- 1 small Korean sweet potato (goguma), about 6 oz
- 8 oz tteokbokki rice cakes (10–12 pieces)
- 6–8 perilla leaves (kkaennip)
- 2 scallions
- ½ cup shredded mozzarella (optional)
- Lettuce and ssamjang, to serve (optional)

### Sauce:

- 2 Tbsp gochugaru (1–1½ Tbsp for less spicy)
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp Korean curry powder
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp rice wine
- ½ Tbsp corn or oligo syrup (or more sugar)
- 2 Tbsp gochujang
- 1 Tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil

### Fried rice (optional):

- 2 servings cooked rice
- 1 Tbsp reserved sauce
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 1 sheet roasted gim, crumbled
- 1 tsp roasted sesame seeds

## Instructions:

1. Soak rice cakes in cold water about 20 min.
2. Mix the sauce. Toss chicken with it (reserve 1 Tbsp for fried rice) and marinate while prepping vegetables.
3. Halve the sweet potato lengthwise, slice thinly on a slight diagonal. Cut cabbage, perilla, and scallion into small pieces.
4. Pan on medium-high (oil optional). Add chicken, sweet potato, rice cakes, and cabbage. Cook 5 min, stirring occasionally. Lower the heat if the rice cakes stick or brown too fast, or add about ¼ cup water.
5. Add perilla and scallion, cook 3–4 min more until rice cakes and sweet potato are soft and chicken is cooked through. Add cheese with 2–3 min left.
6. Fried rice: reheat the leftovers on medium with sesame oil. Add rice and reserved sauce, stir about 5 min, breaking up clumps and scraping up the brown bits. Stir in gim, finish with sesame seeds.
