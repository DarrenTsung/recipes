## Notes

Serves 6. (Could use [chili oil](/chili_oil) in place of step 1.)

## Ingredients:

- ½ cup oil, divided
- 1–2 fresh Thai bird chilies, thinly sliced
- 6–8 dried red chilies, roughly chopped
- ½–1½ Tbsp Sichuan peppercorns, ground (reserve ¼ tsp for garnish; ½–1 tsp for milder)
- 3 Tbsp ginger, finely minced
- 3 Tbsp garlic, finely minced
- 8 oz ground pork
- 1–2 Tbsp doubanjiang (spicy bean sauce)
- ⅔ cup low-sodium chicken broth (or water)
- 1 lb silken tofu, cut into 1" cubes
- ¼ cup water
- 1½ tsp cornstarch
- ¼ tsp sesame oil (optional)
- ¼ tsp sugar (optional)
- 1 scallion, finely chopped

## Instructions:

1. Heat ¼ cup oil on low. Add fresh and dried chilies, stir occasionally about 5 min until fragrant, not burnt. Set aside with the oil.
2. Remaining oil on medium. Add ginger, then garlic after 1 min, fry 1 min. High heat, add pork, break up and cook through. Add ground Sichuan peppercorns, stir 15–30 sec without burning.
3. Add doubanjiang, stir well. Add broth, simmer about 1 min. Mix water and cornstarch.
4. Stir in the cornstarch slurry, bubble until thickened (splash of water if too thick).
5. Add the chili oil with its peppers, then tofu. Gently toss, cook 3–5 min. Add sesame oil, sugar, and scallion, stir until the scallion just wilts.
6. Sprinkle with the reserved Sichuan peppercorn powder.
