---
title: Shrimp Pesto Pasta
---

## Source

<https://www.dinneratthezoo.com/shrimp-pesto-pasta/>

## Ingredients:

- 10 oz spaghetti, linguine, or fettuccine
- 1 lb peeled and deveined shrimp
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper
- 3/4 cup basil pesto
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1/4 cup grated parmesan

## Instructions:

1. Cook pasta in salted water according to package directions.
2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a large pan over high heat. Add shrimp, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper; cook 2–4 minutes, until just pink and opaque.
3. Drain pasta and add it to the shrimp. Stir in pesto, tomatoes, and parmesan.
