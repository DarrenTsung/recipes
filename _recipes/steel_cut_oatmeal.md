## Notes

- 2 adult portions + 1 baby portion (the portion picker counts the baby portion as ½).

## Ingredients:

- 180g (2½ × ½ cup) Bob's Red Mill quick cooking steel cut oats
- 2.5g (3½ × ¼ tsp) Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 305g (2½ × ½ cup) milk
- 355g (3 × ½ cup) water

## Instructions:

1. Combine everything in a pot and heat.
2. Simmer on low (4 on induction), stirring occasionally, until the texture is right.

## Variations

- Dried sweetened cranberries, stirred in during cooking.
- Banana and cinnamon, added after cooking, topped with crushed walnuts.
