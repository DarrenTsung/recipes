## Source

Woks of Life cookbook, p. 231

## Notes

Serves 2–4.

## Ingredients:

### Sauce (per batch):

- ~90g (¼ of a batch) [fish fragrant sauce](/fish_fragrant_sauce)
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch (with water)
- 2 tablespoons minced garlic (from ~6 cloves; or frozen garlic cubes)
- 2 teaspoons minced fresh ginger (or frozen ginger cubes)
- 1 Thai chili, minced
- ⅛ teaspoon white pepper powder

### Stir-fry:

- 1 pound Japanese or Chinese eggplants (2 to 3 long eggplants)
- 4½ tablespoons neutral oil
- 4 ounces ground pork or chicken (optional)
- 2 scallions, finely chopped

## Instructions:

1. **Sauce:** Dissolve cornstarch in hot water, then stir in the fish fragrant sauce, garlic, ginger, Thai chili, and white pepper. Set aside.
2. Cut eggplants on an angle, rotating with each cut, into triangular bite-size chunks (don't leave out too long or they'll brown).
3. Wok over medium-high until lightly smoking. Add 1½ tbsp oil, spread eggplant in a single layer, reduce to medium, cook 3-4 min, flipping occasionally, until lightly browned. Add another 1½ tbsp oil, cook 4 more min until seared, soft, and slightly translucent (do in batches if needed). Remove and set aside.
4. High heat, add remaining 1½ tbsp oil. Add pork (if using), cook until opaque, breaking up as you go.
5. Return eggplant to wok, stir-fry to combine. Re-stir sauce, add to wok, simmer until it coats a spoon. Stir in scallions, serve.
