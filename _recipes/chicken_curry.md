## Notes

Serves 4, over rice. (Golden Curry cubes are salty, so the bouillon is kept light. Better the next day.)

## Ingredients:

- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- ~700g boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
- 1 large onion, cut into wedges
- 2 carrots, cut into chunks (rolling cut)
- 2 medium potatoes (Yukon Gold), cut into large chunks
- 600g (2½ cups) water
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon (Better Than Bouillon or 1 cube)
- 4 cubes Golden Curry (half a 220g box, medium hot)

## Instructions:

1. Oil in a large pot over medium-high. Brown chicken, ~5 min.
2. Add onion, cook ~5 min until softened.
3. Add carrots, potatoes, water, and bouillon. Bring to a boil, skim foam.
4. Cover, simmer on low ~15 min until potatoes and carrots are fork-tender.
5. Turn off heat. Break in curry cubes, stir until fully melted.
6. Back on low, simmer ~5 min, stirring often (it catches on the bottom), until thick.
