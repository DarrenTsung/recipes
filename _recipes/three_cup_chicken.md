## Notes

Serves 4–6. (Less water than the original, sesame oil moved to the end, no scallions.)

## Ingredients:

- 25g neutral oil
- ~15g ginger (about 6 slices)
- ~25g garlic (about 5 cloves), sliced
- 1 dried red chili, halved
- ~1kg chicken wings, split at the joint, tips discarded (or ~800g pre-cut drumettes and flats)
- 30g warm water
- 60g Shaoxing wine
- 12–30g dark soy sauce (more for a darker color)
- 25g light soy sauce
- 8g sugar
- ~15g Thai basil leaves (a small bunch)
- ~4g sesame oil (about 1 tsp), to finish

## Instructions:

1. Wok on high with the neutral oil. Add wings in a single layer, sear until golden on both sides.
2. Drop to medium, add ginger, garlic, and chili, stir-fry about 1 min until fragrant.
3. Add water, Shaoxing wine, both soy sauces, and sugar. Stir and cover.
4. Medium heat, simmer 15 min (20 for more tender). Stir once or twice (less liquid, so check it isn't catching).
5. Uncover, high heat, reduce a few minutes, stirring often, until the sauce is dark, glossy, and clings to the chicken.
6. Add Thai basil, toss about 1 min until wilted. Off heat, drizzle in the sesame oil and toss.
