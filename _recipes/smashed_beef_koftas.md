## Source

https://www.recipetineats.com/smashed-beef-kofta-with-cucumber-yogurt-sauce/

## Notes

Serves 4. ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6CzTILbld1c))

(a little sweet with the full 2 tbsp cranberries, so halved; skip them and add a squeeze of lemon for less sweet)

(grated onion keeps the beef juicy and spreads onion flavor evenly; easier: blitz it in a mini chopper, or swap 1½ tsp onion powder + 2 tbsp water)

## Ingredients:

### Kofta:

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 500g beef mince
- 1 large onion, grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 tsp sweet paprika
- 1½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- ¼ cup pine nuts (not walnuts, Nico is allergic)
- 1 tbsp dried cranberries or raisins

### Yogurt sauce:

- 1 large Lebanese cucumber, grated
- 1½ cups (375g) full-fat plain yogurt
- 3 tbsp finely sliced mint, plus extra to garnish
- ½ tsp kosher salt

### Serving:

- 1 tbsp pomegranate molasses (or balsamic glaze)
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Warm pita

## Instructions:

1. Mix cucumber, yogurt and mint; set aside.
2. Mix grated onion with everything else for the kofta except oil, pine nuts and cranberries.
3. Heat oil in a large non-stick pan over medium-high. Pinch beef into rough 1-inch chunks straight into the pan (don't shape).
4. Leave undisturbed 5–7 min until deep golden underneath.
5. Flip, add pine nuts and cranberries, stir ~1 min until cooked through.
6. Stir salt into yogurt sauce, spread on a plate, pile beef on top. Drizzle with pomegranate molasses and olive oil, scatter mint, serve with pita.
