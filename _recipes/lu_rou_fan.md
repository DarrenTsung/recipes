## Notes

Serves 4. About 2 hours, mostly simmering.

## Ingredients:

- 450g (1 lb) skin-on pork belly, cut into ½" pieces
- 2 tsp oil
- 25g rock sugar (or 1½ Tbsp granulated sugar)
- 1 small onion or a couple of shallots, diced
- 8 shiitake mushrooms, diced into ½" pieces
- 60ml (¼ cup) Shaoxing wine
- 45ml (3 Tbsp) light soy sauce
- 30ml (2 Tbsp) dark soy sauce
- 475ml (2 cups) water
- 1 premade braising spice packet (滷包; [Han Con Spice for Spiced Food](https://www.instacart.com/store/99-ranch-market/products/2594618-han-con-spice-for-spiced-food-1-7600-oz?retailerSlug=99-ranch-market) from 99 Ranch)
- 4 hard-boiled eggs, peeled (optional)
- Steamed white rice, to serve


## Instructions:

1. Blanch pork belly in boiling water 1 min. Drain and rinse off the scum.
2. Wok on low with the oil. Add sugar, cook until it starts to melt. Add onion, turn to medium-high, stir-fry 1 min. Add mushrooms, stir-fry a couple more minutes.
3. Add pork, Shaoxing wine, both soy sauces, and water. Bring to a boil. Add spice packet and eggs, drop to lowest heat, simmer 1½ hours, stirring occasionally.
4. Pork should be fall-apart tender. Remove the spice packet. Medium-high, stir about 5 min until the sauce coats a spoon but is still plentiful. Serve over rice.
