## Source

What's for Dessert (Claire Saffitz)

## Notes

Makes 1 loaf. Metal 4½ × 8½" loaf pan (measured across the top), hand mixer. Total ~1 hr 40 min plus cooling.

Keeps up to 4 days well wrapped at room temp. Stand mixer works (paddle; rub zest and sugar in the bowl by hand first).

Other pan: 9" round, buttered and bottom-lined. Don't coat the pan with the pistachio/sugar; sprinkle all of it over the batter. Start checking at 35 min.

## Ingredients:

- 300g (1¼ cups) whole milk
- 75g (½ cup) coarse-ground polenta
- 4g (1 tsp) vanilla extract
- Butter for the pan
- 79g (⅔ cup) raw pistachios
- 163g (1 tbsp + ¾ cup) sugar, divided
- 169g (1¼ cups) all-purpose flour
- 6g (1½ tsp) baking powder
- 3g (1 tsp) Diamond Crystal kosher salt (or ½ tsp Morton)
- 1 tsp finely grated lemon zest
- 141g (10 tbsp) unsalted butter, room temp
- 100g (2 large) eggs, room temp

## Instructions:

1. Bring milk to a simmer in a small saucepan over medium-high. Slowly sprinkle in polenta, whisking constantly, until thick like porridge and holds whisk marks, ~3 min (should bubble if left alone). Off heat, whisk in vanilla. Scrape into a medium bowl and cool completely, uncovered, stirring occasionally to prevent lumps.
2. Brush pan with room-temp butter. Line bottom and long sides with parchment, 1–2" overhang each side. Butter the parchment.
3. Bash pistachios in a sealed bag with a rolling pin until the largest pieces are lentil-size. Shake through a mesh sieve over a medium bowl; keep the larger pieces in the sieve.
4. Toss 13g (1 tbsp) sugar with the fine pistachio bits. Sprinkle several generous pinches into the pan, tilting to coat. Reserve the rest for the top.
5. Rack in center, preheat oven to 350°F.
6. Whisk flour, baking powder, and salt.
7. In a large bowl, rub lemon zest into remaining 150g (¾ cup) sugar until fragrant, like wet sand. Add butter, beat on medium-low to combine, then medium-high until pale and fluffy, ~4 min, scraping occasionally.
8. Beat in eggs one at a time on medium-high, then beat until very light, thick, and creamy, ~2 min more.
9. Medium-low: add ⅓ of the dry, mix until combined. Fold the cooled polenta a few times to loosen lumps, add half, mix on medium until lump-free. Add remaining dry in two additions, alternating with remaining polenta, until smooth. Fold in the larger pistachio pieces with a spatula, scraping bottom and sides.
10. Scrape into pan, work into corners, smooth. Sprinkle with reserved pistachio/sugar. Bake until risen and split, the split looks dry, and a skewer in the tallest part comes out clean, 50–60 min.
11. Cool in pan 20 min. Run a knife down the short sides, lift out by the parchment, cool completely on a rack.
