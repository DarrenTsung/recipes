## Notes

Serves 2.

## Ingredients:

- 1 package (12 oz) sundubu (extra soft / silken tofu)
- ⅓ cup thinly sliced kimchi (fully fermented)
- 3 oz pork or beef (or chicken, seafood, mushrooms)
- 1 scallion, finely chopped
- 1 egg (optional)

### Seasonings and broth:

- 1-3 tsp gochugaru
- 2 tsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp sesame oil (a little more if using more gochugaru)
- 1 cup water or anchovy broth
- 2-3 tbsp kimchi juice
- ½ tsp saewujeot (salted shrimp) or salt, to taste
- Pinch of black pepper

## Instructions:

1. Cut meat and kimchi into small thin strips. Add to a small pot with gochugaru, garlic, and sesame oil over medium heat.
2. Stir-fry 3-4 minutes, until meat is nearly cooked.
3. Add water/broth and kimchi juice. Boil 3-4 minutes, skimming scum.
4. Add tofu in large chunks. Stir in saewujeot or salt (start with ¼ tsp) and black pepper. Cook 4-5 minutes.
5. Add scallion just before removing from heat. Crack in egg (optional) and serve boiling hot.
